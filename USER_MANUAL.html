01 · Getting started

Quick start & sign-in

Use a current browser and the account details supplied for your role. Student accounts are created by a JEN Academia administrator after a learner completes a trial outside the platform and signs a contract.

New learner

Contact JEN Academia to get started

  1. Email jenacademia31@gmail.com or send a message using the contact form on the public JEN Academia website.
  2. Tell the team about your learning goals and ask about arranging a trial class. The trial is conducted outside the platform.
  3. After the trial, review the available class contract with the team.
  4. If you decide to continue, sign the contract. An administrator will then create your student account.
  5. Use the secure account setup link the administrator sends you to finish your profile and set your password.
No public registration or trial booking: new learners cannot create an account or book a trial from the platform.
Existing user

Sign in

  1. Choose Log in. At the role-choice screen, select Student / Teacher or Admin.
  2. Enter the email and password associated with your account.
  3. If you forgot your password, choose Forgot password, enter your email, and follow the link sent to your inbox.
  4. At your first sign-in, complete any requested profile setup. Then your role-specific Home page opens.
Teacher and admin accounts: these accounts are created by an administrator. Ask your school administrator for your account details if you have not received them.

02 · Your account

Profile & account settings

Keep your contact details current so scheduling and account messages reach you. The Account page may show different profile fields for learners and teachers.

  1. Open Account from the navigation menu or profile menu.
  2. To change your profile picture, choose the picture controls and select an image or use the camera if your device offers it.
  3. Choose the edit option in your profile information, update the fields you need, and save.
  4. When asked to verify a phone number, request the code and enter it in the verification dialog.
  5. To change your password, open the Security section, enter your current password and a new password, then save.
  6. Use Log out when you finish on a shared or public device.

Time zone

Check your account time zone and keep it correct. Dates and class times are displayed using account and schedule settings.

Teacher profile

Teachers can review their teaching profile and other role-specific details on the Account page. Ask an administrator if a managed field needs correction.

Automatic sign-out: the application signs out an inactive session after five hours. Sign in again to continue.
Privacy: use your own account. Do not share your password or leave your account open on a device other people use.

03 · Learner guide

Student / learner

Calendar, booking & class day

Use Calendar to see your schedule, book an available class, or ask your teacher to change a scheduled class.

Book a class

  1. Open Calendar, then choose Book a class.
  2. Select a date marked as available. Availability depends on your teacher's schedule and existing bookings.
  3. Choose an open time and check the teacher, course, date, and time shown.
  4. Select Book it to submit the booking.
  5. Return to Calendar and confirm the class appears in your schedule.

Ask to reschedule

  1. In Calendar, open the class you need to change and choose the reschedule option.
  2. Select a date and time that are available. The calendar checks for time conflicts.
  3. Enter a short reason (at least five characters), review the request, and send it.
  4. Watch your notifications for your teacher's decision. The original class time stays in place until the request is approved.
Important: sending a request does not move the class. Do not assume the new time is confirmed until the other participant approves it.

Join your scheduled class

  1. Open the class from your Calendar at the scheduled time.
  2. If prompted, read the recording notice. The recording is used for attendance verification, is visible to the admin and assigned class participants, and is automatically deleted after seven days.
  3. Choose I Consent and Join to continue, then choose Open Meeting.
Before class: check the class date and time on the calendar and allow your browser to open the meeting in a new tab.

04 · Learner guide

Student / learner

Assignments, feedback & lessons

Complete an assignment

  1. Open Assignments and choose a task.
  2. Read the instructions and due date.
  3. Type your answer or attach the requested file.
  4. Review your work and choose Submit.
  5. Reopen the task to check its status. If editing is available, use the edit option.

Read teacher remarks

  1. Open Remarks from the menu.
  2. Read your teacher's notes, starting with the most recent.
  3. Use the feedback to prepare for your next class.

Open a book or lesson

  1. Open Books/Lessons and select a book.
  2. Choose a lesson to open its reading material.
  3. Read through the lesson. Your reading place is saved when you leave.
  4. When you finish, choose Mark as Complete. The next lesson may then unlock.

Review your class history

  1. Open Class history to see past classes.
  2. Choose a class to review its details and available class recording.
  3. Recordings are temporary and are deleted automatically after seven days.

Share a contract review

  1. Open Contract reviews when a review is waiting for you.
  2. Select the relevant review and rate each question using the scale shown.
  3. Add a comment if you wish, then read your answers again.
  4. Submit the review. Submitted reviews cannot be changed.
Missing a book or assignment? Only materials made available to your account appear in these pages. Ask your teacher or the school administrator to check that the item was shared with you.

05 · Teacher guide

Teacher

Schedule & student management

Your teacher menu includes Home, Calendar, My students, Class history, Contract reviews, Remarks, Assignments, and My books.

Set your teaching availability

  1. Open Calendar and choose Set Availability.
  2. Select a day and enter the hours you can teach.
  3. Add a break if you are unavailable during part of that time.
  4. Choose Save Availability. Saved times appear as available for student booking.
  5. Review your calendar regularly so your published availability matches your schedule.

Find and support students

  1. Open My students to see the learners assigned to you.
  2. Use the search field to find a student by name.
  3. Review the course, package, and classes remaining shown for that student.
  4. Use the student selection in Assignments or Remarks to choose the correct learner.

Handle a reschedule request

  1. Open the notification panel from the notification bell.
  2. Review the student, current class time, requested time, and reason.
  3. Choose Approve or Reject. If you reject, provide a reason when asked.
  4. Check Calendar to confirm the request status and class schedule.
Teacher and student approvals: reschedule requests are decided by the class participants. Administrators can view requests but cannot approve or reject them.

06 · Teacher guide

Teacher

Teach, verify & record a class

Complete the class workflow from its Calendar entry. Class verification may require the attendance recording and a written summary.

During and after class

  1. Open the scheduled class from Calendar.
  2. Review the class time and join the meeting using Open Meeting.
  3. When ready to capture attendance evidence, return to the class page and choose Start Recording. Stop and upload the recording when finished.
  4. Write a class summary with at least one complete sentence.
  5. Return to the class details and add a short, helpful class remark for the learner.
  6. Choose End, Verify & Complete when the class is over, the recording has finished uploading, and the required information is ready.
  7. Check Class history to confirm the completed class is listed.
Recording consent: both class participants are asked to consent before joining. Explain the notice and do not continue unless the required consent is given. Recordings support attendance verification and are automatically deleted after seven days.

Write a useful class summary

Describe what happened in the session, such as the topic covered and the learner's participation. Use a complete sentence and avoid unnecessary sensitive personal details.

Leave a learner remark

Share one concrete strength and, when useful, one next step. Remarks are visible to the student and help them prepare for their next lesson.

Assignments, reviews & books

  • In Assignments, choose a student, create a task, add instructions and an optional due date or file, then post it. Use submissions to review the student's work.
  • In Contract reviews, score each required skill, add a helpful note, and review the computed level before submitting.
  • In My books, open a book shared by the administrator, read its lessons, and use the progress view to check student progress.
Books are read-only for teachers. Administrators manage the shared library and its lessons. Contact an administrator if a book needs to be added or assigned to your class.

Create an account

  1. Choose Create Student or Create Teacher in the admin menu.
  2. Complete the required identity, contact, course, schedule, and account fields presented by the form.
  3. For a student, select the assigned teacher and course/package information as appropriate.
  4. Review the details carefully and submit the form.
  5. Provide the new user with their sign-in instructions privately.
Privacy and permissions: use the correct role and assigned teacher. Teachers should only access their assigned students and class data. Avoid sharing accounts or passwords.

08 · Administrator guide

Administrator

Manage books, lessons & reviews

Build and share the book library

  1. Open Books / Lessons in the Admin Dashboard.
  2. Choose the option to add a book. Enter its title, description, course, and any optional author or cover information requested.
  3. Select the teacher or teachers who should receive the book, then save it.
  4. Open the book's content area to add lessons and upload each lesson's learning material.
  5. Check the book and lesson list, then confirm that assigned teachers can see the book.
  6. Use the book's teacher distribution controls to change which teachers have access.

Update, archive, or restore a book

  • Edit the book details or cover from the library controls.
  • Archive a book to remove it from the active library while preserving the option to restore it later.
  • Use the archived view and restore action when a book should become active again.
  • Hard deletion is not available. Archive is the safe, reversible way to retire a book.
Access model: admins own and maintain the shared library. Teachers can read books assigned to them and review their students' progress. Students can read the books available through their assigned teacher and course.

Review progress and completed evaluations

  • Open the book's progress view to check lesson completion for students.
  • Use Contract reviews to inspect submitted evaluations and feedback.
  • Use Verifications to review attendance evidence and class verification status.
Before changing access: confirm the intended teacher and course. Removing a teacher's distribution removes their access to that book and its lessons.

09 · Alerts & communication

Notifications & LINE reminders

Use in-app notifications

  1. Choose the notification bell to open your notification panel.
  2. Read each alert and open the related class or request.
  3. If you are asked to decide on a reschedule, review the date, time, and reason before approving or rejecting it.
  4. Return to Calendar to check the current schedule after a decision.

Connect LINE reminders (optional)

When LINE reminders are configured for the school, a learner can connect a LINE account from the Account page.

  1. Open Account, find the LINE reminders card, and choose Connect LINE.
  2. Use the add-friend link or search for the school's LINE account shown on screen.
  3. Send the displayed six-digit linking code to the official LINE chat within ten minutes.
  4. Wait for the Account page to show that the connection is complete.

Pending requestA follow-up may arrive while a request is still awaiting a decision.

Pause or disconnect: use the LINE reminders card in Account to pause messages while keeping the link, or disconnect LINE. Reconnect if you have changed LINE accounts.
No LINE card or no message? LINE messages are optional and depend on school setup. Check your connection and reminder settings. Contact the school administrator if the feature is not available.

10 · Help & good practice

Common issues & glossary

If this happens…Try this
You cannot sign inCheck the email and password, confirm the correct role entry, and use Forgot password if needed.
The page sends you back to sign-inYour session may have expired or your account may need attention. Sign in again; contact the administrator if the issue continues.
No booking time is availableTry another available date. Teachers publish their own availability and already-booked times cannot be selected.
Your reschedule is still pendingThe other class participant must approve it. Keep the original time in your calendar until a decision is made.
A lesson or book is missingAsk the teacher or administrator to check that the right course and teacher have access to it.
A file will not upload or openCheck your internet connection, confirm the file is the one requested, and retry. If it still fails, ask your teacher or administrator for help.
The meeting link is unavailableReturn to Calendar and reopen the scheduled class. If the link is still missing, contact the school administrator.
You cannot see an admin or teacher menu itemMenus depend on your account role and permissions. Sign in using the correct account or ask the administrator.

When you need help

Write down the page you were using, what you expected to happen, and the message displayed. Share those details with your teacher or school administrator. Never send your password or a private sign-in code.
